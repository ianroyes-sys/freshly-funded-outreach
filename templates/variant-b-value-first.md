# Variant B — value-first

For companies scoring **8+**. Costs 30–45 minutes per company. Several times the reply rate
of Variant A, because it inverts the transaction: the sender gives before asking.

**This is the variant that gets interviews.** Cap it at 2–3 per week and do them properly.

## The principle

A junior candidate has no name recognition and no warm intro. The only currency he has is
**work already done**. A founder who receives something genuinely useful, with no strings,
replies — even when they aren't hiring — because it's rare and it's flattering and it costs
them nothing to answer.

## What to actually make

Pick **one**, sized to the company:

| Deliverable | Best for | Sender's angle |
|---|---|---|
| **Onboarding teardown** — sign up, note the first 10 minutes | Any product with self-serve signup | He's shipped consumer apps; he knows where people drop |
| **Pricing / packaging read** | Fintech, SaaS, B2B | Finance major — this is literally his training |
| **Unit-economics sketch** | Marketplaces, commerce, SMB | Modeling is his strongest hard skill |
| **3 growth ideas with the reasoning** | Creator tools, media, consumer | $30K in ad sales = he's actually sold to real buyers |
| **Competitor scan** | Crowded categories | Fast, and shows judgment |

**Rules for the artifact:**
- Published as its own page — a clean link, not a PDF, not an attachment
- **Specific and falsifiable.** "Your pricing page buries the free tier below the fold" beats
  "your pricing could be clearer"
- 3 points, not 10. A wall of criticism reads as arrogance from a stranger
- Genuinely useful even if they never reply — that's what makes it not a trick
- **No flattery padding.** Founders can smell it

## Shape

```
subject: <what you made, lowercase, no pitch>

Hi <first name> —

<1 sentence: saw them in StrictlyVC, so you went and used the product. Concrete.>

<3 short specifics — the actual findings. Bulleted or semicolon'd, no preamble.>

Wrote it up properly here: <link>

<1 sentence: no ask attached to that — it's yours.>

<2 sentences: separately, here's who I am, one number, the availability.>

Jordan Lee · jordanlee.example.com
```

**Hard limits:** 120–160 words (slightly longer than A is fine — you've earned it).

## Worked example

> **subject:** pulled apart your onboarding — 3 notes
>
> Hi Maya —
>
> Ledgerline came up in StrictlyVC so I signed up and ran through it as if I were the bookkeeper
> at a 12-person company.
>
> Three things in the first 10 minutes: the QuickBooks connect step asks for admin scope
> before explaining why, and I'd bet that's your biggest drop; the empty state after import
> shows zero of the reconciliation value; and pricing doesn't say what happens when I cross
> 500 transactions, so I assumed the worst.
>
> Wrote it up properly here: jordanlee.example.com/basis
>
> No ask attached to that — it's yours either way.
>
> Separately: I'm a finance senior at Lakeside State who ships. Ran university ad sales from zero to
> $30K a semester and built Pocketbook, a personal-finance app that's live. If you're hiring a
> generalist this year I'd like to be in that conversation — part-time now, full-time June.
>
> Jordan Lee · jordanlee.example.com

## Checklist
- [ ] The artifact is **real, published, and linked** — the loop may not draft this email
      until the teardown page actually exists
- [ ] Three specifics, each falsifiable
- [ ] The gift is explicitly unconditional ("no ask attached to that")
- [ ] The ask is separate, second, and small
- [ ] Under 160 words
- [ ] Nothing claimed that isn't in `profile/profile.md`
