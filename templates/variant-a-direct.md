# Variant A — the direct ask

For companies scoring **6–7**. Cheap to produce, honest, gets a reply from founders who are
actively hiring a generalist. Use Variant B for anything scoring 8+.

## Shape

```
subject: <lowercase, specific, 4–7 words, no pitch>

Hi <first name> —

<1 sentence: something only someone who actually looked would know.
 Their product decision, their positioning, who led the round and why that's interesting.
 NEVER "congrats on the raise.">

<2 sentences: who the sender is, framed as capability. One number. The "finance kid who ships" idea,
 said in his own words, not as a slogan.>

<1 sentence: the ask. Name the role shape, not a job title they haven't posted.>

Jordan Lee · jordanlee.example.com
```

**Hard limits:** 75–125 words. One link. No attachments. No signature block art.

## Worked example

> **subject:** finance kid who ships — 15 min?
>
> Hi Maya —
>
> Saw Ledgerline raised $6M led by a seed fund. What got my attention is that you went at the
> bookkeeping close instead of the invoicing layer everyone else is crowded into.
>
> I'm a finance senior at Lakeside State. I ran ad sales for our student newspaper and built it from
> zero to $30K a semester, and I build software on the side — my personal-finance app
> Pocketbook is live. So I'm the finance-brained one who can also ship the thing.
>
> Seed companies usually need one generalist doing revenue ops, modeling, and whatever's on
> fire that week. If that's on your list, worth 15 minutes? I'm part-time now and full-time
> from June.
>
> Jordan Lee · jordanlee.example.com

## Subject lines that work
- `finance kid who ships — 15 min?`
- `your <specific feature> + a finance student who builds`
- `generalist for <company>?`
- `<company> + revenue ops — worth 15 min?`

## Subject lines that get deleted
- ~~`Passionate About Joining Your Mission!`~~
- ~~`Congratulations on your Series A!`~~
- ~~`Experienced Finance Professional Seeking Opportunity`~~
- ~~Anything in Title Case With Every Word Capitalized~~

## Banned openers
- "Congrats on the raise!" / "Congratulations on the funding!" — **the single clearest tell**
- "I hope this email finds you well"
- "I'm reaching out because..."
- "My name is Jordan and I am a..."

## Checklist before this draft is allowed to exist
- [ ] Under 125 words
- [ ] Opening line proves he looked at the actual product, not just the funding line
- [ ] Exactly one number from `profile/profile.md`
- [ ] One ask, one link, zero attachments
- [ ] Founder's real first name, spelled right
- [ ] Nothing claimed that isn't in `profile/profile.md`
